# MT Deck Builder

Build complete, presentation-ready decks for Modern Trade analytics storytelling.
Every deck follows a proven narrative arc — never create slides that are just data dumps.

---

## Deck Types

| Deck | Audience | Slides | Tone | Trigger |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Monthly Leadership Review** | VP/CMO/CFO | 10–12 | Executive, crisp | "monthly review deck" |
| **QBR (Quarterly Business Review)** | Leadership + NKAM | 15–18 | Strategic, data-driven | "QBR deck", "quarterly review" |
| **NKAM Chain Review** | Account team | 8–10 | Operational, action-oriented | "NKAM deck", "chain review" |
| **Channel Launch** | Leadership + Sales | 10–12 | Persuasive, growth-focused | "launch deck", "new channel" |
| **Investor / Business Case** | Board/Finance | 12–15 | Financial, ROI-focused | "business case", "board deck" |
| **Annual MT Strategy** | Leadership | 18–20 | Visionary, roadmap-oriented | "annual plan", "strategy deck" |

---

## Narrative Arc (Mandatory — All Decks)

Every deck follows this 5-chapter arc. Never skip or reorder:

```
Chapter 1 — SITUATION (What's the context?)
  → Market position, FY overview, chain landscape

Chapter 2 — COMPLICATION (What's the problem/opportunity?)
  → Gap vs target, declining chains, growth opportunity

Chapter 3 — INSIGHT (Why is it happening?)
  → Root cause: distribution, listing, DOS, pricing, execution

Chapter 4 — RESOLUTION (What's the plan?)
  → Chain-specific interventions, investment cases, priorities

Chapter 5 — COMMITMENT (What's the ask / next step?)
  → Budget, headcount, approvals, timeline, milestones
```

## Slide-by-Slide Template: Monthly Leadership Review (10 slides)

### Slide 1 — Executive Flash (Title Slide)
**Content:** Month label + FY tag, key headline metric
**Formula:** `[Month] MT Performance: NSV [₹XL], [+/-X%] vs [LY/Target]`
**Speaker notes:** Lead with the number; let the headline answer "are we winning?"
**Visual:** KPI tile strip — NSV | GM% | Trade Spend% | Key wins

### Slide 2 — Dashboard Scorecard (Health at a Glance)
**Content:** 6–8 KPIs in traffic-light format
```
KPI             | Actual | Target | LY    | vs Target | vs LY
NSV (₹L)        | 142.3  | 150.0  | 118.6 | -5.1%     | +19.9%
GM%             | 38.2%  | 40.0%  | 36.5% | -1.8pp    | +1.7pp
Trade Spend%    | 18.4%  | 18.0%  | 17.1% | -0.4pp    | -1.3pp
Numeric Dist%   | 72.1%  | 75.0%  | 68.3% | -2.9pp    | +3.8pp
```
**Visual:** Heatmap table — green/amber/red cells; no raw data without traffic lights.
**Speaker notes:** "We're on track vs LY but 5% short of target — distribution gap is the single biggest lever."

### Slide 3 — Chain Performance Matrix
**Content:** Top chains ranked by NSV contribution + growth rate
**Visual:** Bubble chart (X = NSV, Y = YoY growth, size = GM%), OR ranked bar with LY overlay
**Speaker notes:** Identify 3 categories:
- Champions (NSV ↑, Growth ↑): Protect and scale
- Value Traps (NSV ↑, Margin ↓): Flag for trade spend review
- Recovery Plays (NSV ↓, Opportunity ↑): Lead with action plan

### Slide 4 — Offtake & Consumer Pull
**Content:** Offtake value vs Primary NSV by chain — Primary-Offtake Gap analysis
**Headline formula:** `"At [Chain], DOS is [X] days vs optimal [Y] — trade stock build of ₹[Z]L"`
**Visual:** Waterfall — Primary NSV → Offtake → Gap → Inventory implication
**Speaker notes:** Offtake tells consumer demand truth; gap tells channel efficiency. High gap = stocking, not selling.

### Slide 5 — Brand × Pack Performance
**Content:** Top brands + SKUs by offtake velocity, distribution, and sell-through
**Visual:** 2×2 matrix (X = Velocity, Y = Distribution) with bubble = NSV
**Categories:**
- High Velocity + High Dist: Core (protect)
- High Velocity + Low Dist: Expand (listing priority)
- Low Velocity + High Dist: Rationalize (range review)
- Low Velocity + Low Dist: Exit or support

### Slide 6 — Distribution & Listing Health
**Content:** Numeric + Weighted distribution trend + listing rate vs targets
**Visual:** Dual-axis line (Numeric Dist% + Weighted Dist%) + bar for new listings
**Speaker notes:** Distribution is a leading indicator — where we're listed today predicts NSV 4–6 weeks out.

### Slide 7 — P&L Waterfall
**Content:** NSV → GM → Trade Spend → Channel EBITDA by chain
**Headline formula:** `"GM% at [X%], [±Y]pp vs last month. Primary driver: [trade loading/returns/COGS]"`
**Visual:** Horizontal waterfall chart — green bars (revenue), red bars (deductions/costs), EBITDA highlight

### Slide 8 — Root Cause Deep Dive (One Key Issue)
**Content:** Pick the single most important underperformance — diagnose with the 10-driver sequence
**Format:** 3 boxes — WHAT (the metric gap) → WHY (the root cause) → SO WHAT (the business impact)
**Visual:** Decision tree or fishbone with 3 confirmed root causes highlighted

### Slide 9 — Actions & Owner Grid
**Content:** 5–7 prioritized actions with chain, owner, deadline, expected NSV impact
```
| Priority | Action | Chain | Owner | By | NSV Impact |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | List [SKU] in [Chain] | BigBasket | NKAM | 15 Sep | +₹8L |
```
**Visual:** Priority-colored table (P1=red, P2=amber, P3=green)

### Slide 10 — Forward Look: Next Month Outlook
**Content:** Target for next month, confidence level, key assumptions, risks
**Format:** `"[Month] target: ₹[X]L [P50]. Key assumption: [seasonal uplift / new listing / DOS normalization]. Risk: [Reliance billing timing]"`
**Visual:** Bar chart — target vs optimistic vs conservative scenarios

## Slide-by-Slide Template: QBR (15 slides)

### Chapter 1 — Situation (3 slides)
1. **QBR Headline** — Quarter in 3 numbers + 3 bullets
2. **FY Progress** — YTD NSV vs target, FY trajectory
3. **Chain League Table** — All chains ranked, QoQ movement

### Chapter 2 — Complication (3 slides)
4. **NSV Gap Analysis** — Where did we miss? Chain/brand/month breakdown
5. **Consumer Demand Signal** — Offtake trend + Primary-Offtake Gap by chain
6. **Distribution Health** — Distribution % vs target, whitespace map

### Chapter 3 — Insight (3 slides)
7. **Root Cause #1** — Biggest NSV underperformance + 10-driver diagnosis
8. **P&L Health** — GM%, Trade Spend%, EBITDA by chain
9. **Market Share Signal** — Honasa share vs category, MoM trend

### Chapter 4 — Resolution (3 slides)
10. **Chain Action Plans** — P1/P2/P3 interventions per chain with owner
11. **SKU Priority for Q3** — New listings + rationalization recommendations
12. **Trade Investment Case** — Where to increase spend (ROI >1.5), where to pull back

### Chapter 5 — Commitment (3 slides)
13. **Q3 Target Build** — Bottom-up NSV target by chain + brand
14. **Milestone Calendar** — Key execution milestones with owners
15. **Ask / Approval Required** — Budget, headcount, or policy approvals needed

## Speaker Notes Formula

For every slide, write speaker notes with this structure:
```
[Opening hook — 1 sentence with the most surprising number]
[Explanation — 2 sentences WHY this happened]
[Action bridge — 1 sentence connecting to next slide or action]
```

Example:
```
"BigBasket NSV grew 42% YoY but our offtake only grew 11% — we've stocked the chain, not sold to consumers.
DOS at BigBasket is 38 days vs our target of 21, which means ₹18L of inventory is sitting idle.
The next slide shows the three SKUs with the worst sell-through, which are the listing rationalization candidates."
```

## Data Viz Recommendations by Slide Type

| Slide | Recommended Chart | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Scorecard | Heatmap table | Traffic lights communicate status instantly |
| Chain ranking | Horizontal bar with variance arrow | Shows both size and direction |
| Primary vs Offtake | Waterfall or grouped bar | Shows components of gap |
| Distribution trend | Dual-axis line | Two related metrics, one trend |
| P&L waterfall | Horizontal waterfall (NSV → EBITDA) | Shows value erosion visually |
| Growth matrix | 2×2 bubble | Classifies chains/SKUs into strategy quadrants |
| Offtake map | Heatmap (chain × month) | Highlights weak periods quickly |
| Forecast | Grouped bar (actual + scenarios) | Shows uncertainty range |

## Pitch Deck Principles (for Business Case / Board Deck)

**Structure every pitch with:**
1. **Problem** — The pain your chain partner or business is experiencing (not about you)
2. **Solution** — What MT Analytics / your recommendation delivers
3. **Unfair Advantage** — What makes the Honasa/MT approach unique
4. **Business Model** — How it generates financial return (NSV, GM%, EBITDA)
5. **Proof** — Specific chain case study with numbers
6. **The Ask** — Budget, approval, timeline

**Pitch delivery rules:**
- Get to the number in the first 60 seconds
- One headline per slide — not a title, a conclusion
- 45-minute sessions: 30 min deck + 15 min Q&A
- Repeat questions before answering (in large rooms)
- Prepare backup slides for every key metric you show

## Workflow to Build a Deck

```
Step 1 — Define
  - Deck type (QBR / Monthly / NKAM / Launch)
  - Audience (VP / CMO / NKAM / Board)
  - Key message (the single most important point they should remember)
  - FY + month scope (e.g., FY27 Jul-26 Monthly Review)
  - Target chain(s) if chain-specific

Step 2 — Gather Data Blocks (from dashboard/data.js)
  - NSV, GM%, Trade Spend%: primary block
  - Offtake, DOS: offtake block
  - Distribution: dist block
  - Forecast: forecast block

Step 3 — Build Narrative Arc
  - Situation (2 sentences): "In [Month], MT NSV was ₹[X]L, [+/-Y]% vs LY"
  - Complication (2 sentences): "However, [Chain] underperformed by ₹[Z]L due to [cause]"
  - Insight (1 sentence): "Root cause: [distribution gap / DOS build / listing miss]"
  - Resolution (1 sentence): "Action: [specific intervention, owner, timeline]"
  - Commitment (1 sentence): "Ask: [₹X investment / listing of Y SKUs / policy change]"

Step 4 — Populate Slides
  Use the slide templates above.
  For each slide: title (as a conclusion, not a label) + bullets + visual + speaker notes

Step 5 — QC Before Sharing
  □ Every number cited matches dashboard/data.js or source workbook
  □ Every slide has a clear "so what"
  □ Speaker notes have the hook-explain-bridge structure
  □ No slide with more than 5 bullets
  □ Consistent FY labels ("FY27 Jul-26", not "July 2026")
  □ No NaN or undefined values in any metric
```

---

## Anti-Patterns (Never Do)

- Title slide that just says the company name — always add the key metric
- Slides full of raw data tables without traffic lights or trend lines
- Ending a slide without a "so what" conclusion
- Mixing FY naming conventions (FY27 vs FY2027 vs 2026-27)
- Charts without axis labels or units
- Speaker notes that just repeat the bullet points
- Showing NSV without context (vs target, vs LY, vs chain benchmark)
- Deck that ends without a clear ask or next step
