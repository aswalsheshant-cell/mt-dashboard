# Proactive Intelligence Engine

Build rapid, actionable market intelligence for Modern Trade—competitor moves, market trends, 
category dynamics, and strategic recommendations grounded in data and clear business implications.

## Core Methodology: CONTEXT → DRIVERS → IMPACT → ACTION

Every intelligence brief follows this four-step framework. Each is non-negotiable:

### CONTEXT (The Market Picture)
**What is the current state?**
- Define the market, segment, or competitive set
- State time window (e.g., "Q1–Q2 FY27", "last 6 months", "since last review")
- Name key players (competitors, new entrants, adjacencies)
- Include market size (if known) and growth trajectory
- Example: "Personal Care in Modern Trade: ₹4,200L NSV market, growing 11% YoY. 
  Mamaearth: 6.4% share (₹270L). Top 3 competitors: HUL (28%), P&G (15%), ITC (9%)"

### DRIVERS (Why It Matters)
**What changed to shift the landscape?**
- Identify 2–4 primary drivers (not a list of 10):
  - **Competitive moves:** New launches, pricing changes, distribution expansion, trade spend
  - **Category dynamics:** Mix shift, pack size trends, seasonal patterns, channel migration
  - **Market factors:** Regulatory, macro, consumer behavior, supply chain
  - **Your business impact:** What this means for your sales, margin, share, positioning
- Use data to isolate cause from correlation
- Example: "HUL launched budget range (₹299, -30% vs core) in Modern Trade. Driven by 
  value-conscious consumer trend (Nielsen shows 18% category share shift to <₹400). 
  Impact: Price realization risk in Mamaearth premium lines (Hair, Skincare); potential 
  -120 bps share if we don't respond"

### IMPACT (The Business Stake)
**What does this mean for Mamaearth/the category?**
- Translate market moves to business outcomes:
  - **Short-term:** Sales risk, margin pressure, share loss, volume cannibal
  - **Medium-term:** Positioning risk, channel share erosion, customer list loss
  - **Strategic:** Competitive threat level (red/amber/green), market share trajectory
- Quantify where possible (e.g., "-₹15L NSV if we don't respond"; "3–5 pt share at risk")
- Flag upside opportunities (white space, adjacency)
- Example: "If HUL captures 8% Modern Trade share (currently 28%) with budget range, 
  Mamaearth could lose ₹18–22L NSV (4–5 pt share decline). Mitigation: Launch sub-₹500 
  line by Sep (3-month runway). Upside: +2–3 pt share if we own the value tier"

### ACTION (The Play)
**What should we do?**
- Propose 1–3 decisions or immediate steps:
  - **Defense:** Counter-move, tactical response, repricing
  - **Growth:** White-space play, adjacency, partnership
  - **Insight:** What data to collect, where to dig deeper
- Tie each action to the IMPACT (not random initiatives)
- Include: timeline, resource need, success metric, owner
- Example:
  - **Decision 1:** Launch sub-₹500 Hair Care SKU (₹3L capex; 6-week timeline; 
    success: +₹12–15L NSV in first quarter; owner: Category Head)
  - **Decision 2:** Reallocate 20% Modern Trade trade spend to value segment 
    (trial Oct–Nov; measure: market share hold in budget tier; owner: Trade)
  - **Decision 3:** Capture competitive pricing data weekly via retail audit 
    (low cost, high signal; success: early warning of next move; owner: Field Sales)

## Response Hierarchy by Scenario

### Market Report (What's the Market Doing?)
**Structure:**
1. Market size + growth (your segment vs. category vs. MT channel)
2. Top 3 competitor moves (launches, pricing, distribution, trade spend)
3. Market share landscape (leader, challenger, niche positions)
4. Emerging trends (mix shift, price ladder migration, channel dynamics)
5. Implication for Mamaearth + 2–3 recommended plays

**Length:** 400–600 words (executive brief format)

**Lead with:** Single highest-impact move (e.g., "HUL launched budget range; threatens 
₹18–22L share if unresponded")

### Competitor Overview (Who Are They, What Are They Doing?)
**Structure:**
1. Competitor profile: market share, NSV, growth rate, positioning (budget/core/premium)
2. Recent moves (last 6 months): launches, pack sizes, SKUs, pricing, distribution
3. Trade spend strategy: discount depth, volume schemes, loyalty programs
4. Strengths vs. Mamaearth (where they beat us, why)
5. Weaknesses (where we have an edge)
6. Threat level + recommended response

**Length:** 300–500 words

**Lead with:** Competitive threat level (red/amber/green) and share-at-risk number

### Due Diligence Brief (Should We Enter/Expand/Partner?)
**Structure:**
1. Market opportunity: size, growth, white space for entrant
2. Competitive barriers: incumbent strength, customer loyalty, distribution moats
3. Your fit: where we have advantage, what we'd need to build, time to breakeven
4. Downside risks: category risk, macro headwinds, regulatory
5. Go/no-go recommendation + conditions for success

**Length:** 500–700 words

**Lead with:** Recommendation (Go, Go with Conditions, No-Go) + rationale in 1 sentence

### Industry Brief (Macro/Category Trends)
**Structure:**
1. Macro trend: regulatory, consumer behavior shift, supply chain, channel migration
2. Category impact: winners vs. losers, margin pressure, volume opportunity
3. Modern Trade specific: how MT is repositioning, what channels are growing
4. Mamaearth position: where we're positioned for the trend vs. at risk
5. Action items: monitoring, capability gaps, investment opportunity

**Length:** 350–500 words

**Lead with:** Headline trend + Mamaearth implication (positive/negative/mixed)

## Competitive Intelligence Checklist

Before finalizing any brief, verify you've covered:

- [ ] **Data sourced:** Nielsen, Retailer feedback, Audit, Public info, or clearly flagged as 
  "reported" (not stated as fact if unverified)
- [ ] **Timeframe clear:** "Last 6 months", "Q1 FY27", "since March 2026" (not vague)
- [ ] **Competitor actions quantified:** Price point (₹299), SKU count (launched 3 new), 
  distribution breadth (500 stores), not descriptive ("aggressive", "major")
- [ ] **Implication stated in business terms:** NSV at risk, share decline, margin pressure, 
  customer loss—not vague ("threat", "opportunity")
- [ ] **Action tied to impact:** Why this play addresses the move
- [ ] **Success metric defined:** What we measure to know if the play worked
- [ ] **Owner assigned:** Who makes the decision, who executes

## Metric Language for Intelligence Briefs

| Situation | ✓ Say This | ✗ Don't Say |
|-----------|-----------|------------|
| Competitor launched new SKU | "HUL launched ₹299 shampoo; targets value tier; expect 2–3 pt share if unresponded" | "HUL launched a competitive product" (vague) |
| Market share moved | "Competitor gained 180 bps in Modern Trade (28% → 29.8%) in Q1; driven by new store openings (+80 locations)" | "Competition is intense" (no data) |
| Price pressure | "Retail price realization fell 45 bps due to 20% promo mix increase by HUL" | "Price is under pressure" (no cause) |
| Entry opportunity | "Premium segment (>₹600) growing 18% YoY; white space in Natural/Organic; 3-month runway to capture" | "There's an opportunity in premium" (no specifics) |
| Category risk | "Value tier (<₹400) gaining 18% share (vs core -8%); if trend continues, ₹45L NSV at risk in Mamaearth premium by FY28" | "Value is a threat" (no quantification) |
