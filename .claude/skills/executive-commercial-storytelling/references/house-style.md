# House style — decks, summaries, email and trackers

The organisation's existing conventions. Match them; they are what make output
paste-ready rather than something that needs reformatting before use.

## QBR and leadership deck conventions

- **One strong title per slide, uppercase.** It states the finding, not the category.
- **KPI tiles directly below the title** — the two or three numbers that prove the title.
- **Coloured section ribbons** to separate blocks of the deck.
- **Green up, red down** trend markers. Semantic only, never decorative.
- **"Others" hidden in charts but included in totals.** A chart showing the top eight
  chains must still foot to the full total; state that the balance sits in Others.
- **Record highlights immediately below the table** they refer to, not on a later slide.
- Absolute values in ₹ Cr to one decimal; growth as a signed percentage to one decimal;
  Indian digit grouping; the unit in the header, not in every cell.

## Deck section order

Title → KPI tiles → exception section (what needs attention) → action table. Every deck
closes with the action table. A review that ends on analysis rather than actions
produces no decisions.

## Executive summary

Three to four lines. Not five. It is written last and read first, and it must be
actionable on its own.

```
MT NSV closed at Rs 12.4 Cr, +8.2% YoY, at 94% of plan.
Growth was concentrated in DMart and Reliance, which together contributed 78%.
Health & Glow declined 14% on a 118-store listing gap worth Rs 42 L a month.
Ask: NKAM to confirm listing restoration dates for Health & Glow by 25 Aug.
```

## Ready-to-use sentence patterns

Fill the brackets; keep the shape. These are the phrasings the organisation already
uses, so they read as familiar rather than as a new format.

**Performance**
- `[Chain] delivered Rs [x] Cr NSV in [month], [+/-y]% YoY, at [z]% of plan.`
- `[Category] grew [x]% against a category growth of [y]%, [gaining/losing] [z] bps of share.`
- `Growth was concentrated in [chains], contributing [x]% of the total increase.`

**Exception**
- `[Chain/Article] declined [x]% MoM, driven by [cause], not by demand.`
- `[n] stores that billed in [base month] dropped to zero in [month], worth Rs [x] L.`
- `DOI in [chain] stands at [x] days against an agreed cover of [y] — Rs [z] L of ageing stock.`

**Opportunity**
- `Closing the [n]-store listing gap on [article] in [chain] is worth Rs [x] L a month.`
- `At [y]% of the rate of sale in existing stores, this adds Rs [z] Cr annualised.`

**Action**
- `[Owner role] to [specific step] by [date]; visible in [metric] from [month].`

Avoid: "monitor closely", "needs attention", "sales were impacted", "due to various
factors". Each of these replaces a cause with a gesture at one.

## Action table — closes every deck

| Column | Content |
|---|---|
| Issue | One line, names the chain, article or geography |
| Impact | ₹ value per month, and annualised only if structural |
| Action | The specific step |
| Owner | Role: NKAM `<chain>`, RKAM `<zone>`, Category, Supply, Trade Marketing, Analyst |
| By when | A date |
| Review | When the effect becomes visible, and in which metric |

## Email and WhatsApp

- Polished corporate wording. Calm, direct, no filler. Not lengthy.
- Always offer a subject line for an email.
- Lead with the conclusion or the ask, not the background.
- One ask per message. Two asks halve the response rate on both.
- Numbers in the body, detail in the attachment — never the reverse.
- For WhatsApp: shorter still, no greeting paragraph, and no attachment that needs a
  desktop to open without saying so.

## Plans, OKRs and trackers

Any plan or tracker carries these columns, presented as a clean table the user can paste
straight into Excel or PowerPoint:

Objective · Key Results · KPIs · Owner · Timeline · Status · Next Action · Review Rhythm

## Quality bar before sending

- Does the output have backing source data and master mapping logic behind it?
- Are QC checks included or referenced?
- Is there an exception view, not just totals?
- Is it paste-ready for office use without reformatting?
- Is the explanation brief and to the point?
- Does every number on the artifact carry a source and a period?
