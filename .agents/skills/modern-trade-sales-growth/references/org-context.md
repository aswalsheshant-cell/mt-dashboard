# Organisation context and vocabulary

Shared constants for every Modern Trade skill in this suite. Use these names and terms
verbatim — matching the organisation's own vocabulary is what makes output paste-ready
instead of needing translation.

Referenced by `modern-trade-sales-growth`, `demand-inventory-planning`,
`sales-data-reconciliation`, `executive-commercial-storytelling`,
`business-ai-automation` and `retail-execution-tracking`.

## Who the work serves

The user is an **MT (Modern Trade) Channel Analyst Lead** in FMCG, running a structured,
accurate, timely, insight-driven reporting system across Primary Sales, Offtake, DMS,
Merchandising, Contest, QBR and Master Data.

Six work buckets:

1. Reporting accuracy and timeliness
2. Offtake and business analytics
3. QBR and leadership review
4. DMS, contest and merchandising tracking
5. AI and process improvement
6. People management

Stated priorities: speed, accuracy, ownership, simplicity, continuous improvement.

Working style to match: explain simply first, then give the final format; keep it brief
and result-focused; make every output directly usable in mail, Excel, PPT, a tracker,
a QBR or a leadership update. Ask only the few clarifying questions genuinely needed to
avoid wrong output — never questions already answered in the conversation, and none at
all when the task is simple and unambiguous.

## Standard analytical flow

**Assortment → Primary → Offtake → Insight → Action.**

Work the stages in that order. Skipping to offtake without checking assortment produces
a demand explanation for what is actually a listing gap.

## Views to always consider

Chain, Zone, State, Category, Sub-Category, Article (SKU), Pack Size, Chain Format.

## Metric vocabulary

Use the organisation's term on the left. The right column is what the same measure is
called elsewhere, given only so the two are not treated as different metrics.

| Term | Definition | Also known as |
|---|---|---|
| **NSV** | Net sales value | — |
| **ASP** | Average selling price = NSV ÷ Units | Realisation per unit |
| **DOI** | Days of inventory — closing stock ÷ average daily offtake | Days of supply, days of cover |
| **L3M** | Last three months, used as a smoothed comparison base | Trailing 3-month average |
| **TDP** | Total distribution points | Weighted distribution |
| **MTD / QTD / YTD** | Month, quarter, year to date on the Indian FY | — |
| **Plan achievement %** | Actual ÷ target | Achievement, versus plan |
| **Offtake** | Consumer-side sale out of the store | Sell-out, secondary |
| **Primary** | Sale into the chain or its distribution centre | Sell-in, dispatch |
| **MRN** | Material return note — a returns document; a negative NSV line usually traces to one | — |
| **SIS** | Shop-in-shop | — |
| **BC** | Brand counter (a Reliance data status value; `non brand counter` is a different value) | — |

**DOI is the standard term in this organisation.** Where a skill in this suite says
"days of supply", read it as DOI and write DOI in any output.

## Standard dashboard conventions

Typical slicers: Month, Chain, Zone, State, Chain Format, Sub Category, Article.

Typical KPIs: NSV, Units Sold, ASP, Plan Achievement %, YoY Growth %, DOI.

## Fiscal year

Indian FY, April to March. April–December of year Y is FY(Y+1); January–March of year Y
is FY(Y). Derive it from month and year; never hardcode a year list. Sort months in
fiscal order — April first.
