# CM2 two-level dashboard design

Date: 2026-10-03
Repository: `aswalsheshant-cell/mt-dashboard`
Status: written design for owner review; no dashboard implementation or B5 sign-off

## Intent and approved direction

Decision makers need CM2 figures that respond to the dashboard's selected period and chain, reconcile from signed source rows, and disclose which costs are actually loaded. After reviewing the owner's local `MT_Brand_wise_PL_FY27_Provision_vs_Actual_Claims v2.xlsx`, the owner approved its two views: **provisioned claims** and **recorded MT Direct debit-note claims**. They are alternative valuations of overlapping commercial events, never additive. HTML and Power BI must report the same amount, basis, coverage, and status. No source data, signed returns, or approved totals may be removed to make a view reconcile.

The design avoids calling a modeled or incomplete cost amount “actual.” The workbook's `Data_Provision` has 133 provision claim rows totaling ₹1,174.3635 lakh and 40 provision BA rows totaling ₹769.6355 lakh. Its `Data_ActualClaims` has 64 recorded direct-DN rows totaling ₹1,274.7132 lakh. The provision amount is a planning input; the direct-DN amount is recorded source evidence, with July and August marked partial. Both resulting CM2 views remain provisional because COGS/logistics come from assumptions, cost coverage differs, and Finance has not signed a complete margin scope. **GST is recorded source data**, not one of those assumptions, where the matching Primary invoice tax and base are present and valid. Draft PR #267's separate Q1 distributor-chain claim control (₹449.7694 lakh, with provisional monthly allocation) is a future source under HOLD; it is **not** part of either workbook view and must not be added until overlap, alias and approval decisions are recorded.

## Current evidence and first failing boundaries

The main expense input has 64 real FY27 Apr–Aug MT Direct DN rows totaling ₹1,274.7132 lakh before rounding; all July and August rows are marked `PARTIAL MONTH`. It omits distributor claims and actual BA, COGS/logistics and other missing cost heads. FY27 all-channel NSV is about ₹22,239.90 lakh, including ₹1,163.96 lakh of non-MT sales; MT-only raw NSV is ₹21,075.94 lakh. The workbook's 340 `Data_Primary` rows equal the repository's rounded `dashboard/data.js` MT detail rows exactly at month × source-chain × brand, totaling ₹21,074.56 lakh. Its sales base is therefore ₹1.38 lakh below raw MT CM2 NSV because `scripts/build_dashboard_data.py` rounds each grouped detail row to two decimals before serialization. Preserve unrounded source values for calculation; expose a rounding bridge rather than adjusting either source.

The local workbook is not checked into the named Claude branch and has no cached formula results, so its P&L totals have **not** been observed after an Excel recalculation. Its formulas allocate chain-month claim and BA amounts to brands by primary NSV share; that is modeled attribution, not a brand-coded actual claim. Provision claims cover ₹20,508.59 lakh of its ₹21,074.56 lakh sales base (97.31%). Actual DN claims cover ₹11,357.57 lakh (53.89%). Five `North RMTs` provision chain-months total ₹137.9529 lakh without matching primary sales; two `WH-Smith` direct-DN chain-months total ₹2.20 lakh without matching sales. Both belong in explicit unallocated reconciliation. The workbook's `PL_Overall` percentage-gap formula `=(Z6-P6)*100` subtracts two CM2 percentages with different covered-NSV denominators, so it is not a valid like-for-like margin gap. Its claim-amount gap uses matched coverage but the percentage gap does not.

The owner's GST correction is supported by the checked-in FY27 Apr–Aug Primary article CSVs: all 123,773 inspected rows have numeric `Inv. Net value(LOC)` and `Inv. Tax Amount(LOC)`; 97,506 rows have tax/net rates near 18%, 25,774 near 5%, six have zero recorded tax, and 487 need row-level review (many have very small invoice bases, so do not label them invalid merely from a rounded ratio). The monetary weighted tax/net ratios vary by month: April 15.17%, May 14.86%, June 14.39%, July 13.85%, August 14.20%, across all channels in those source files. These are **diagnostic source ratios**, not a new approved CM2 GST rate or MT-only reconciliation. `Total MRP sales` is also present and is distinct from invoice net value. The existing TOT pipeline already retains row tax and uses it when `Avg Tot` is unavailable; CM2's workbook formula must not reintroduce a flat 18% GST rate.

The builder's rollup excludes nonpositive NSV groups at `scripts/build_dashboard_data.py:3188-3191`, so signed chain/category sums differ from the headline while the negative source rows remain. It also serializes a FY26+FY27 CM2 of ₹53,865.55 lakh / 97.7% and FY26 month values where no expense exists. HTML hides the mixed-FY KPI, but the payload still offers it to consumers. HTML's headline cards respond to FY only; the chain table responds to Chain; Month, Brand and Category do not affect the CM2 section. The August MoM card displays a −₹1,168.78 lakh loaded-cost CM2 movement across two partial expense months without a local caveat. Power BI's manual CM2 DAX kit is not installed in the checked-in one-page PBIP, and its FY-only comparability gate does not test partial-month coverage. The daily Date Table has repeated `MonthStart` values although the manual relationship guide requires uniqueness.

## Canonical source and calculation contract

Create a shared, versioned CM2 reporting fact with one row per source-event ID and governed grain: fiscal year, calendar month, canonical chain, channel, expense head, source type (`PROVISION_CLAIM`, `PROVISION_BA`, or `DIRECT_DN_ACTUAL`), source period, signed amount in INR lakh, source status, timing status, approval reference, and overlap key. Keep raw customer/distributor identity and file/row lineage alongside canonical chain. Preserve negative sales and negative claim adjustments. All joins must be many-to-one at their declared keys; unmapped and no-sales claim items remain in explicit QC/unallocated buckets and are never silently assigned to another chain. The user workbook is an input to validate, not a substitute for governed source lineage; its formula-derived chain alias and brand allocation require independent checks.

The NSV denominator comes from the same signed Primary article population as the current CM2/TOT calculation, with the channel basis explicit. Compute two bases for explanation: **MT-only** and **all-channel**. The primary leadership view for MT Direct claims uses MT-only where channel classification is supported; all-channel stays a separate labeled comparison until Finance approves its use for CM2. Never combine one base's NSV with another base's costs while calling the result a margin. A signed negative-NSV chain/month still appears in the reconciliation detail even when its percentage is unavailable or economically hard to interpret.

Both workbook views need the same governed pre-claim base at supported MT invoice/article × month × chain grain. Use the Primary row's `Inv. Net value(LOC)` as the invoice NSV field, `Inv. Tax Amount(LOC)` as **actual billed tax**, and `Total MRP sales` as the separately recorded MRP/gross field, preserving signed credits and the existing distributor allocation weights. Verify each field's units and treatment of returns before use. Where invoice NSV is confirmed tax-exclusive, `effective billed GST = Σ actual tax / Σ invoice NSV` on the selected rows; compute the ratio from monetary sums, never average row rates or use one FY-wide rate for month views. Do **not** treat MRP and invoiced gross as interchangeable or subtract tax from already tax-exclusive NSV. Reconcile source MRP, invoice NSV, tax, and on-invoice trade pass-on at the same grain under the existing TOT source-priority rule before selecting the modeled COGS base. The workbook's `GMV = NSV × 1.18 / (1 − TOT/MD)` is not a governed reconstruction when source tax and MRP exist; replace it only after the gross/COGS base definition is confirmed. The 16% COGS and 3% logistics rates, and 30% default TOT/MD for chains absent from the provision sheet, remain explicit assumptions requiring Finance review. Do not apply these components to the existing `NSV − recorded DN` loaded-cost card without relabeling, and do not call either view reported profit. If any source tax, MRP, NSV, or allocation relationship is incompatible, withhold that modeled CM2 cell while showing the underlying source amounts and QC exception.

Carry `GST_QC_Status` per invoice row into month × chain × article × brand × category reporting: `VALID_5_PERCENT`, `VALID_18_PERCENT`, `MIXED_RATE` (aggregate of individually valid rows), `ZERO_TAX`, `MISSING_TAX`, and `REVIEW_RATE_OR_BASE`. Set validity using a documented rounding tolerance and amount materiality so a ₹0.10 invoice with ₹0.02 tax is reviewed rather than automatically called a 20% tax-rate error. Validate actual tax totals before and after distributor allocation, match the source month, and disclose any missing/review amount. The dashboard may show a weighted effective tax rate for the selected population only after these checks; it must not silently fall back to 18%. A separately governed fallback, if ever needed, must be labeled estimated, not actual.

The first view, **Provision basis — modeled CM2**, uses chain-month provision claims from the owner's sheet. Show provision BA as a separate line and optionally `CM2 after provision BA`; do not merge BA into claims or imply it is paid actual. The second view, **Recorded Direct DN basis — modeled CM2**, replaces the provision claims with validated direct-DN claims for the same chain/month. It does **not** subtract provision claims too. July and August remain `PARTIAL_REGISTER`; claim absence is `NOT_AVAILABLE`, not zero. Both views restrict the CM2 numerator and NSV denominator to their own loaded chain-months and disclose coverage. Amounts with no matching sales remain visible as unallocated; do not force an invented margin denominator.

The recorded direct-DN source must remain separate from modeled cost and attribution columns. The existing `PowerBI/Reference/CM2_Provisional/config/cm2_formula.csv` has a self-certified approval stamp contradicted by the recovered governance notes, so it is not sufficient Finance approval evidence. Brand-level provision and actual claim values may be shown only as **NSV-share allocated estimates**, with an allocation reconciliation and a label that source claims lack brand coding. Category margin stays unavailable until a source supplies category or Finance approves an allocation. The present 100% payload values from blank cost attribution cannot be surfaced as profit.

## Availability, coverage and filter rules

Every reported CM2 cell carries `status` (`MODELED_PROVISION`, `MODELED_RECORDED_DN`, `PARTIAL_REGISTER`, `UNALLOCATED_NO_SALES`, `NOT_COMPARABLE`, or `NOT_AVAILABLE`), the exact months, channel basis, included expense heads, source versions, mapped/unmapped amount, NSV coverage, source-tax QC, modeled-cost assumption version, and approval/timing status. A “Finance actual CM2” status is reserved for a separately approved scope that identifies included and excluded heads, source per head, month coverage, allocation grain and double-count rule. A received claim file or green CI alone does not trigger that promotion.

Compute supported FY×Month×Chain selections from joint signed source data, not from independently summed FY-only and chain-only aggregates. Single and multi-select periods use precisely the selected months for both NSV and costs; mixed periods with missing expense coverage withhold a whole-population CM2 value and percentage while still showing NSV and loaded cost separately. If showing a covered-subset ratio, put its covered NSV and coverage percentage in the same visual. Brand view requires the NSV-share allocation label and reconciliation; category and other unsupported dimensions show an explicit unsupported-scope message and no filtered margin. Exports and tooltips carry the same scope/status as the cards. Do not fall back to a combined FY total when a requested FY has no CM2 data.

Compare provision versus actual only on **identical chain-month/brand allocation keys** and the same modeled CM1 base. The workbook currently has 23 matched chain-month keys with ₹11,280.96 lakh of rounded-detail NSV, ₹721.5595 lakh of provision claims and ₹1,259.7150 lakh of direct DN claims; matched actual minus provision claims is ₹538.1555 lakh. This amount gap is an investigation cue, not an approved economic variance: provision plan basis and actual register timing differ. A percentage-point gap is permitted only if both cuts use that same matched NSV denominator and cost assumptions. Never subtract independent provision and actual covered-subset percentages as the workbook's current `PL_Overall!AC6` does.

Monthly movement compares like-for-like channel, chain, cost-head and status coverage. If either month is partial, withhold the CM2 change headline or label it clearly as **observed NSV and loaded-expense movement, provisional** with both drivers. The current July→August observed arithmetic is NSV −₹1,262.69 lakh, recorded expense −₹93.91 lakh, resulting loaded-cost CM2 −₹1,168.78 lakh; it is not a certified margin trend.

## HTML and Power BI experience

On HTML P&L, add a coverage strip and two clearly separated workbook-aligned CM2 views. Show the selected FY/month/chain, MT/all-channel basis, source-derived tax and its QC, modeled cost assumptions, cost-source status and covered NSV at the point of each KPI, then a signed reconciliation table, provision versus actual claim bridge on matched keys, and a small exception list with partial/unallocated flags. An exception is a prompt for investigation, not a profitability ranking while major costs are modeled or missing. Keep the existing recorded-DN loaded-cost card separately labeled if retained. Keep the existing cohort and other tab numbers unaffected. All cards, chart series, tables, tooltips, drill links and downloads must agree under the same supported filter state.

The checked-in `ModernTrade_Report.pbip` currently has one Finance sample page and no PL Expense Input or CM2 table. Add the governed CM2 fact, a unique date/month relationship, explicit status measures, and a CM2 page to that repository model rather than claiming the manual DAX kit is already active. The page should present the same provision and recorded-DN views, source coverage and matched comparison, with BA provision separate. Manual DAX/Power Query files and QuickSetup guidance must describe the actual assembled model. Live Desktop refresh, relationship cardinality, filtered visual values, and the governed B5 cases are separate runtime evidence gates.
